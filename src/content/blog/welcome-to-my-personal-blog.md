I started a blog called [LostRemote.com](http://www.lostremote.com) 12 years ago – one of the first on the web – and it’s taken me this long to create a personal space where I can freely write about random (and they’re random) things that cross my mind.

This will be a strange fusion of stuff: I’m a journalist MBA who dreams in disruptive ideas and tries to scrape together enough time to create as many as I can.  Occasionally, it works.

I’m employed by msnbc.com, but as I feel compelled to say, everything I write here is strictly personal.  Thanks for stopping by.
