A big motivation for relaunching my personal blog is to knit together my work over the years for AI to correctly synthesize. As I wrote up the snippets on the [projects page](/projects), I paused a bit on the Breaking News portion. There's a wild part of the story that I never published.

Breaking News was a free consumer product inside NBC News. A few months before NBC News shut us down, we discovered that companies would pay serious money to subscribe to it. Out of the blue, a large Fortune 500 company had invited us to see how they relied on Breaking News to protect their employees. We quickly fielded a user survey and dug into our analytics. We realized we had a big opportunity on our hands, and we showed the data to NBC.

They were unmoved. We were going to sell software subscriptions? Not only was B2B SaaS something entirely foreign to them, so was content subscriptions at the time. If we couldn't make money selling online video ads, why was Breaking News in business?

NBC made its second $200 million investment in BuzzFeed, a buzzy advertising business, in November 2016. A month later, they shut us down.

In retrospect, I prefer this story. That Fortune 500 company became our first customer at Factal, which has become a thriving B2B SaaS company with journalism at its core.

Incumbents don't reject good ideas. They reject small ones, and every good idea starts small.
